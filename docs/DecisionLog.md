# Decision Log

---

## Day 0

### Decision

Reframed the project from **"Recommendation CTR Analysis"** to **"Trusted Product Analytics Platform."**

### Why

The original idea focused primarily on explaining why a KPI changed, which naturally leads to an analytics-oriented portfolio.

After reviewing the responsibilities of the Data Product Engineer role and discussing the project direction, it became clear that the core responsibility is not only analyzing business metrics, but ensuring that the underlying data can be trusted.

The new scenario better represents real Data Product Engineering responsibilities, including:

- Event Tracking
- Event Schema Management
- SQL Analytics
- Databricks
- Data Quality
- KPI Validation
- Stakeholder Communication

### Alternatives Considered

**Option A**
Recommendation CTR Analysis

Pros

- Easy to understand
- Simple business story

Cons

- Too analytics-focused
- Limited opportunity to demonstrate Data Quality and Product Engineering

---

**Option B (Selected)**

Trusted Product Analytics Platform

Pros

- Closer to real-world Data Product Engineering
- Naturally includes Event Tracking, SQL, Dashboard, and Data Quality
- Better aligned with the hiring manager's responsibilities

Cons

- Slightly more complex implementation

### Outcome

The entire project will now focus on one central question:

> **Can stakeholders trust the Product Analytics Dashboard?**

Every future module—including Event Design, Synthetic Data, Databricks, SQL, Dashboard, and Data Quality—must support answering this question.
